Trial Reel Poster: Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

Trial Reel Poster is a private publishing tool run by Ritwik Chakradhar for the AwareWolf accounts (Instagram aware._wolf_ and its YouTube and TikTok channels). It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

  • Instagram: the account's own reels, their captions and insights, and permission to publish reels.
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos and manage playlists on the AwareWolf channel.
  • TikTok (optional, later): the account's basic profile and permission to publish videos.

How it's stored

  • Sign-in tokens are encrypted and stored on Cloudflare. They are used only to post to the AwareWolf accounts.
  • Video files are kept only until they are posted and deleted 7 days after posting.
  • Post history (dates, links, status) is kept so the team can track what went where.

What it never does

  • It doesn't sell, share or rent any data.
  • It doesn't access anyone else's account, messages or followers' personal data.
  • It doesn't use data for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions, Instagram's apps and websites settings, or TikTok's manage-app-permissions page. Stored tokens are then useless and are deleted.
