Growth Playbook

Instagram

  • Reach: trial reels are the free test on non-followers. Only winners get promoted, and they're graduated within 24 h.
  • Reach: write for sends. Instagram has said shares by DM are a top signal for reaching new people. End on a line people forward (“Send This To The Friend Who…”).
  • Reach: searchable name field and caption first line, plus the keyword said and shown in the first 3 seconds.
  • Engagement: reply to every comment in the first hour. Pin a question. A weekly reply-reel answers the best comment.
  • Engagement: a Story every day you post, and a poll every Wednesday. Broadcast channel weekly.
  • Following: 3 pinned reels that show the range. “Start Here” highlight. Monthly Collab post with Mia or a peer, which shows on both profiles.
  • Following: keep your keyword-DM CTA (ManyChat) because a DM thread is a relationship, not a view.

YouTube

  • Reach: backfill best-first, so the first Shorts YouTube tests are your strongest. Hook as the title, under 60 characters, one keyword.
  • Reach: clean files only (the API copy has no watermark), 2 a day at fixed slots, music swapped where flagged.
  • Engagement: reply to every comment, pin a question, heart the good ones.
  • Following: playlists per series and a related video on every Short, so one view leads to the next. Channel trailer = your best reel.
  • Following: subscribe watermark on. Once the backfill is done, 1 long-form a month from existing footage (optional, your call).

TikTok

  • Prerequisite: an account TikTok doesn't treat as Indian. Without it, nothing below works.
  • Reach: keyword in the first line, said out loud, 3 to 5 specific hashtags. TikTok is used like a search engine for wellness.
  • Engagement: reply to comments with video, and stitch a myth to correct it with a named study.
  • Following: series playlists on the profile, 3 pinned videos, and turn Instagram carousels into Photo Mode posts.
  • Labels: AI-generated content label on every reel with AI visuals.
